##### Summary

We estimate the costs and benefits of protecting California's coast from flooding as sea levels rise. Using USGS CoSMoS flood projections for seven pilot sites, we compare nature-based options, such as dunes and artificial reefs, with hard structures, such as seawalls. Dune restoration is usually the most cost-effective option, but the results vary a lot from site to site, so adaptation plans need to fit local conditions.
